# Financial Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze financial sales performance. It provides insights into sales, profitability, cost of goods sold (COGS), discounts, product performance, customer segments and country level trends to support data driven business decision making.

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales - 20.95M
- Total Profit - 2.91M
- Profit Margin - 13.9%
- Total Units Sold - 203.33K
- Total COGS - 18.04M
- Total Discounts - 1.78M
- Total Gross Sales - 22.73M
- Average Sale Price - 118.43
- Average Profit Per Unit - 14.30

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard includes analysis of,

- Monthly sales trends
- Profit performance by country
- Sales performance by product
- Profit performance by customer segment
- Sales distribution by customer segment
- Discounts by discount band
- Profit performance by product
- Country-level sales performance
- Product and segment filtering
- Financial performance across selected date ranges

## Key Insights

- Paseo generated the highest sales among the products displayed.
- Paseo also produced the highest profit among the analyzed products.
- Government represents the largest customer segment by sales.
- High discount levels account for the largest discount amount among the discount bands.
- Mexico recorded approximately 2.9M in profit for the selected dashboard view.
- The dashboard enables comparison of sales, profit, discounts and product performance across countries and customer segments.
