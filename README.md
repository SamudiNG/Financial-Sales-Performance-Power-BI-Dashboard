# Financial Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Financial Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard analyzes sales revenue, profitability, cost of goods sold (COGS), discounts, product performance and customer segments across different countries.
The objective is to transform financial sales data into actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to,

- Monitor overall financial sales performance using key business KPIs.
- Evaluate revenue, profitability, costs and discount performance.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
- Compare sales and profitability across countries and customer segments.
- Analyze monthly sales trends to understand changes in business performance.
- Assess discount distribution and its potential impact on profitability.
- Enable interactive analysis to support data-driven business decisions.

## Dashboard Analysis

The Financial Sales Performance Dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of business performance through the following areas,

1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Monitors total sales revenue and units sold.
- Analyzes monthly sales trends to identify changes in revenue over time.
- Compares sales performance across products and customer segments.
2. Profitability Analysis
- Evaluates total profit and overall profit margin.
- Compares profitability across countries, products and customer segments.
- Measures average profit generated per unit sold.
3. Cost and Discount Analysis
- Examines the relationship between gross sales, discounts, COGS and net sales.
- Analyzes discount amounts across different discount bands.
- Supports the identification of areas requiring further cost and discount evaluation.
4. Product Performance Analysis
- Compares sales revenue and profitability across products.
- Helps identify products with strong sales and profit contributions.
- Supports product-level performance evaluation.
5. Geographic Performance Analysis
- Compares profit contributions across different countries.
- Highlights geographic differences in financial performance.
- Enables country-specific analysis through interactive filtering.
6. Customer Segment Analysis
- Evaluates sales distribution and profitability across customer segments.
- Identifies differences in segment-level financial contributions.
- Supports customer segment performance comparisons.
7. Interactive Business Analysis
- Allows users to filter results by date range, country, product, and customer segment.
- Updates KPIs and visualizations dynamically based on selected filters.
- Supports data-driven exploration and financial performance monitoring.

## Key Insights

- Product-level sales and profit visualizations help identify the products contributing most to business performance.
- Country-level profit analysis highlights differences in profitability across geographic markets.
- Customer segment analysis provides visibility into revenue contribution and profitability by segment.
- Discount band analysis helps identify where discounts are concentrated.
- Monthly sales trends support the identification of revenue fluctuations over time.
- Comparing sales, COGS, discounts and profit provides a broader understanding of financial performance.
- Interactive filtering enables deeper investigation of business performance across countries, products, segments and time periods.
